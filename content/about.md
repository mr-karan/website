I'm Karan — you might stumble across `@mrkaran` in some corners of the internet. I work at [Zerodha](https://zerodha.com/) where I focus mainly on backend engineering along with handling infrastructure and observability.

I like to [tinker](https://github.com/mr-karan) with stuff around monitoring, networking and distributed systems. When I'm not in the tech zone, I'm probably playing badminton, on a road trip, or experimenting in the kitchen. I've got a soft spot for indie music too, and I've curated some of my favorites in a [Spotify playlist](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/5dvbrw93FgUhsZdbnpF3JM).
